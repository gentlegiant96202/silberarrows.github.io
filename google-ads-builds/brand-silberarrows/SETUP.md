# SilberArrows | Brand — Build & Launch Guide

Account: **612-539-2209** · Landing page: **https://mercedes-benz.silberarrows.com/**

A small, cheap campaign to **defend your own name** — so competitors/dealers can't bid on
"silberarrows" and sit above you, and so brand searchers land on a page you control. Brand clicks
are cheap (usually AED 1–4) and convert well.

Files:
- `keywords.csv` — 2 ad groups (Core brand + Variants/misspellings), Phrase + Exact
- `negative-keywords.csv` — blocks the "Silver Arrows" racing/history meaning + jobs
- `responsive-search-ads.csv` — 1 RSA per ad group

---

## STEP 1 — Create the campaign

New campaign → Objective: **Leads** → **Search**.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign name | `SilberArrows \| Brand` |
| Networks | **Search only** — UNCHECK Search Partners AND Display |
| Locations | **United Arab Emirates** (Presence: people in your locations) |
| Languages | English + Arabic |
| Budget | **AED 30–50/day** (you'll rarely spend it all — brand volume is low) |
| Bidding | **Maximize Conversions**, goal = your `Web Form Lead (gtag)` action. (For pure name-defence, **Target Impression Share → Top of page → 90%, max CPC bid AED 8** also works well.) |
| Conversion goals | Same primary action as the Service campaign (form submit) |

---

## STEP 2 — Import (Google Ads Editor)

1. Import `keywords.csv` then `responsive-search-ads.csv` into the new campaign.
2. Add `negative-keywords.csv` as the campaign negative list (or reuse a shared list).
3. Review → Post.

### Structure
- **Brand – Core** — exact `silberarrows` spellings (your strongest, cheapest terms)
- **Brand – Variants & Misspellings** — `silver arrows`, `silberarrow`, common typos

All Phrase + Exact. No Broad (Broad on brand wastes money on the racing-history meaning).

---

## STEP 3 — Two things that make brand work

1. **Add brand terms as NEGATIVES in your Service campaign** (`silber arrows`, `silberarrows`,
   `silver arrows`) — exact/phrase negatives — so the generic Service campaign doesn't bid on your
   name and inflate its CPC. Brand traffic should only flow to THIS campaign.
2. **Watch the "Silver Arrows" meaning.** "Silver Arrows" is also the historic nickname for
   Mercedes/Auto Union racing cars, so the variants ad group can attract F1/history searchers. The
   negative list blocks the obvious ones — check the search-terms report in week 1 and add any more.

---

## Why bother (quick rationale)
- **Cheap insurance:** if anyone bids on "silberarrows", they can appear above your organic listing.
  A brand campaign keeps you on top for a few dirhams.
- **Higher intent:** people searching your name already want *you* — strong conversion rate, low CPL.
- **Keep it separate:** never blend brand into the Service campaign — brand's cheap, high-converting
  clicks would mask the true performance of your generic service keywords.

> Keep budgets independent. If brand "looks amazing" (it will), don't let that tempt you to judge the
> Service campaign by the same numbers — they're different jobs.
